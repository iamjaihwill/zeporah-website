On the desk

Everything coordination needs. Nothing it doesn't.

  • Queue

    Every household, one view

    Open requests, outstanding acknowledgements, and waiting reasons — with an owner for each, and a flag when there isn't one.

  • Approvals

    Explicit, bounded, expiring

    Sharing requests arrive checked against consent and authority status. Reviewers approve, hold, or deny — with a reason.

  • Updates

    Post once, reach the right people

    Write one update; the family members allowed to see it get it, and you see who has acknowledged it.

  • Notes

    Internal stays internal

    Staff-only notes live apart from the family thread, so nothing is shared by accident.

  • Escalation

    A person, not an automation

    When a caregiver is overwhelmed or a case is unclear, it's routed to someone on your team.

  • Audit

    A record that holds up

    Every sharing decision — actor, purpose, recipient, scope, expiry — written to a log no one can edit.

Beyond hospice

Built to extend across the care network.

Zeporah starts with hospice teams. The same controls are designed to extend to approved hospitals, care professionals, funeral homes, and other services — each scoped to a role, a purpose, and a time limit.

Who pays for Zeporah?

Your organization is the customer. Families always use Zeporah for free.

Does Zeporah replace our EHR?

No. Zeporah sits alongside your EHR and handles family-facing coordination — requests, approved updates, tasks, and messages.

Who decides what a family member can see?

Your team does. Zeporah tracks authority evidence and consent status, applies your rules consistently, and holds anything uncertain for a designated reviewer.

Does Zeporah use AI?

Not on household records today. Summaries, flags, and next steps are built by clear rules from what each person is allowed to see, with a source for every line. Any AI assistance we add will only prepare drafts — never approve a share, grant access, or make clinical or legal determinations — and will go through security review first.

Is Zeporah HIPAA compliant?

Zeporah is built to meet HIPAA requirements, and our formal compliance program is underway. See Trust for where it stands.
