For patients & families

You do not have to do everything today.

Zeporah is a calm, private place to see what matters now, share the load with people you trust, and hear from your care team. It's offered through your care provider, and it's always free for families.

How to get Zeporah

What you'll have

Clear, kind, and in your hands.

Know what to do next

Each day you'll see the few things that need you now, and what can wait. No long checklists, no guessing.

Hear from your care team in one place

Updates and questions live in one thread with your care guide, so you're not waiting by the phone.

Let others help, on your terms

Invite family, friends, or neighbors. Choose what each person can see and do, and change it whenever you like.

Keep important papers together

Store the documents you'll be asked for, and know exactly who has seen them and until when.

Your privacy

Nothing is shared just because someone is family.

Before anything private leaves Zeporah, a person on your care team checks that it's allowed. If something has to wait, you'll be told why in plain words.

  • You choose who's in your circle, and what they can see
  • You can withdraw permission at any time
  • Every share is recorded and ends on its own

Getting Zeporah

Questions families ask.

How do I get Zeporah?

Zeporah is offered through care providers such as hospices. If yours uses Zeporah, your care team will invite you. If they don't yet, ask them about it — or let us know, and we'll reach out to them.

Does it cost anything?

No. Zeporah is always free for families.

Who can see our information?

Only the people you invite, within the role you give them, and the staff caring for your family. Sensitive information is shared only after a person on the care team confirms it's allowed.

Is there a real person I can talk to?

Yes. Your care guide is one message away, and if things feel like too much you can pause non-urgent tasks and ask someone to check in.

What happens after my loved one dies?

Zeporah stays with your family for 13 months, with gentle guidance for the first days and the practical steps that follow, at your pace.

Does Zeporah give medical or legal advice?

No. Zeporah helps you coordinate. Medical questions go to your care team, and legal questions to a qualified professional.

Ask your care team about Zeporah.

If your hospice or care provider doesn't offer Zeporah yet, tell us and we'll reach out to them. Families never pay.
