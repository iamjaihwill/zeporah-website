Controlled sharing

Ten checks, every time.

Before any protected information is shared, every one of these must pass. If anything is missing, conflicting, revoked, or expired, the request is held or denied — never quietly allowed.

Before anything is shared, all ten must pass

  1. Right organization
  2. Signed-in person
  3. Role explicitly allowed
  4. Named purpose
  5. Named recipient
  6. Minimum necessary
  7. Authority reviewed
  8. Consent active
  9. Expiry set
  10. Permanently logged

Prepared, then approved

Zeporah prepares the work. People make the decisions.

What Zeporah prepares

  • Summaries of where a household stands, each line linked to its source
  • Flags for what's missing, waiting, or at risk of slipping
  • A next step for every open item, and a flag when no one owns it
  • A release check on every packet before anything is shared

Built on clear rules you can inspect. No AI model reads household records today; any AI assistance we add will follow the same rule — it prepares, a person decides — and only after security review.

What only people do

  • Approve any share of sensitive information
  • Grant or change access
  • Make clinical, legal, or authority determinations
  • Send anything to someone outside the care team

Principles

How we protect families and organizations.

  • Default-deny

    Access starts closed. A family role is never, on its own, a reason to share sensitive information.

  • Separate organizations

    Each organization's households, staff, and records are kept apart from every other organization's.

  • Minimum necessary

    Shares include only what a named recipient needs for a named purpose, and expire on their own.

  • Consent you can withdraw

    Families can revoke consent at any time, and revocation takes effect immediately.

  • A permanent record

    Every sharing decision is written to an audit log that no one can edit or delete.

  • People decide

    Zeporah tracks authority and consent review status. Clinical and legal determinations stay with qualified people.

  • Plain-language reasons

    When something is held, families are told why — kindly, and without jargon or alarm.

  • Internal stays internal

    Staff-only notes are kept apart from the family thread, so nothing is shared by accident.

What Zeporah is not: an EHR, a clinical documentation system, a legal-authority tool, or a funeral or estate service. We stay in our lane so your team stays in control.
