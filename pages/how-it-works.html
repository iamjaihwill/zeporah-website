The span

From diagnosis to 13 months after.

Zeporah is offered at diagnosis, supports the patient through end of life, and stays with the family for 13 months after a death. The household's circle, preferences, and history carry forward the whole way.

At diagnosis

Plan and prepare

Families
Set preferences, invite trusted helpers, and understand what each role can see.
Care teams
Enroll the household, confirm roles, and start the consent and authority record.

Through end of life

Coordinate care

Families
See today's priorities, receive approved updates, and message the care guide.
Care teams
Work the queue, post updates once, approve requests, and escalate when a family needs more.

13 months after

Carry what comes next

Families
Gentle, ordered next steps, documents, and bereavement support at their own pace.
Care teams
Bereavement follow-up and coordination with approved services, all on the same record.

The trust loop

Zeporah prepares the work. People make the decisions.

Every sensitive action moves through the same four steps. Zeporah does the organizing — by clear rules, with a source for every line — and a person approves anything that shares, grants, or discloses.

  1. 1

    Prepare

    Zeporah organizes open requests, flags what's missing or waiting, and summarizes the record with a source for every line.

  2. 2

    Review

    A person checks consent, authority, purpose, and recipient against your policies.

  3. 3

    Approve

    Nothing sensitive moves without explicit approval and an expiry.

  4. 4

    Record

    Every decision is written to a permanent audit log that no one can edit.

What Zeporah prepares

  • Summaries of where a household stands, each line linked to its source
  • Flags for what's missing, waiting, or at risk of slipping
  • A next step for every open item, and a flag when no one owns it
  • A release check on every packet before anything is shared

Built on clear rules you can inspect. No AI model reads household records today; any AI assistance we add will follow the same rule — it prepares, a person decides — and only after security review.

What only people do

  • Approve any share of sensitive information
  • Grant or change access
  • Make clinical, legal, or authority determinations
  • Send anything to someone outside the care team

The network

Everyone involved, only as far as they're approved.

A transition like this touches many people and organizations. Zeporah connects them through the household's shared picture — each one scoped to a role, a purpose, and a time limit.

  • Patients, families & proxies

    The household at the center, with roles they understand.

  • Trusted helpers

    Friends and neighbors who take on specific tasks or receive updates.

  • Hospice teams

    Care guides, social workers, and clinical leadership on the desk.

  • Hospitals & care professionals

    Approved organizations, designed to connect through the same controls.

  • Funeral homes & approved services

    Receive only what's needed, when the family approves it.
