About

Space to grieve. Support to move forward.

Zeporah creates space for families to care, grieve, and move forward by bringing clarity, connection, and compassionate coordination to serious illness, end of life, and the months after a loss.

Our story

“You do not have to do everything today.”

Zeporah began when our founder, Mariesa Welcome, cared for a beloved aunt through stage 4 cancer — and saw how much of a family's strength goes into searching for answers instead of being present. We're building the calm, clear support she wished her family had, together with the hospice teams who walk beside families every day.

Who we build for

For the people beside the bed, and the people behind the desk.

Families carrying more than they expected, and the care teams who guide them through it. Zeporah is built to make both of their days a little lighter.

Our point of view

Why trust-centered coordination.

01

The hardest part is often the coordination

Care is delivered by skilled people. What breaks down is everything between them: updates, permissions, documents, and who does what next.

02

Families and care teams need different tools

A grieving daughter and a hospice operations lead should not share an interface. They should share a picture — and a set of rules both can trust.

03

Trust is earned in the details

Explicit approvals, bounded sharing, and a permanent record aren't features. They're the reason anyone should let us help.
