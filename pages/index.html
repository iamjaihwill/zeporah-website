The problem

The hardest transition, coordinated by phone tag.

Serious illness pulls in a dozen people and a handful of organizations. None of them share the same picture — so families and care teams feel the same six failures from opposite sides.

  • Repeated storytelling

    Families relive it for every new person.

    Care teams re-take history already taken.

  • Fragmented updates

    Families hear different versions from different relatives.

    Care teams call five people to say one thing.

  • Unclear authority

    Families ask, "Am I allowed to request this?"

    Care teams ask, "Am I allowed to tell them?"

  • Scattered documents

    Families search folders, photos, and inboxes.

    Care teams get requests with no consent trail.

  • Uncertain next steps

    Families don't know what matters today.

    Care teams can't see what's stuck.

  • Too many calls

    Families wait by the phone.

    Care teams lose care time to phone tag.

How Zeporah works

One foundation. Every step accounted for.

Zeporah holds the shared picture across the whole transition, and moves sensitive work through the same accountable loop every time.

  1. At diagnosis

    Plan and prepare

    Preferences, trusted helpers, roles, and consent — set up while there is time to think.

  2. Through end of life

    Coordinate care

    Approved updates, tasks, and one thread between the family and the care team.

  3. 13 months after

    Carry what comes next

    Practical next steps, documents, and bereavement support on the family's timeline.

  1. 1

    Prepare

    Zeporah organizes open requests, flags what's missing or waiting, and summarizes the record with a source for every line.

  2. 2

    Review

    A person checks consent, authority, purpose, and recipient.

  3. 3

    Approve

    Sensitive actions happen only on explicit approval, with an expiry.

  4. 4

    Record

    Every decision is written to a permanent audit log.

Zeporah prepares the work. People make the decisions.

Trust, privacy & permissions

Allow, hold, or deny. Never a guess.

Access starts closed. A family role never unlocks sensitive information on its own. Every share passes the same ten checks, and anything missing, conflicting, or expired is held for a person to review.

Built to meet HIPAA requirements. Our formal compliance program is underway.

How we protect families →

  • Default-deny

    Nothing is visible until a role and consent allow it.

  • Explicit approval

    Sensitive sharing happens only when a person approves it.

  • Bounded by design

    Named purpose, named recipient, minimum scope, and an expiry.

  • A permanent record

    Every decision is logged, and no one can edit the log.
